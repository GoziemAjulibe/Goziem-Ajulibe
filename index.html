ABOUT ME

Hi again 👋🏾,

This is where I tell you I’m passionate about creating seamless, accessible, and user-centered experiences that align with business goals. I know, I knoww – these may sound like fluffy buzzwords, and you’ve heard them before. But beyond the fluff, I thrive on solving problems and I'm excited about building products that make life simpler and more meaningful.

I currently work at GoLemon as the Founding Product Designer, where I lead the design of features that simplify grocery delivery for over 12,000 homes. Collaborating closely with cross-functional teams, I’ve also led the design of tools that improve efficiency, simplify complex workflows, and drive user engagement.

Before GoLemon, I worked at Paystack (a Stripe company), where I contributed to creating seamless payment experiences for businesses and individuals.

Now that we're done with work, here are some non-fluffy things you can know about me: I love a good puzzle. I really like bread (of all kinds). Outside of design, I enjoy reading good books, having conversations with the community inside my head, and watching true crime documentaries that keep me up at night.
