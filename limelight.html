Limelight

Content Guide

When GoLemon (a grocery delivery start-up) transitioned from a 2-day to a next-day delivery service, it became clear that their order fulfillment process, which was previously managed through Google Sheets, was no longer effective. The system was fragmented, prone to errors, and lacked real-time visibility, leading to inefficiencies and communication breakdowns between teams. With the company’s growing scale, the need to streamline and automate the order fulfillment process became urgent.

ROLE

As the sole product designer on this project, I worked closely with a product manager and a team of engineers to design Limelight, an internal tool to centralize communication, streamline workflows, and improve the overall accuracy and efficiency of GoLemon's order fulfillment process.

IMPACT

  • Streamlined Communication: Teams no longer rely on fragmented communication channels like Slack. The Situation Room provides a centralized platform for logging and resolving customer issues, improving collaboration and ensuring nothing is overlooked.
  • Improved Inventory Accuracy: By integrating with the inventory system. Limelight ensures accurate tracking of inventory, even when items are returned by QA. This eliminates stock discrepancies and simplifies reconciliation.
  • Reduced Human Error: Automating key processes, such as payment processing and inventory updates, significantly reduces the risk of human error, ensuring more accurate order fulfillment and better customer service.
  • Increased Scalability: With Limelight, GoLemon has been able to scale its operations to support next-day delivery without sacrificing accuracy or efficiency. The system is flexible enough to handle a larger volume of orders, and the streamlined process makes it easy for teams to manage the increased workload.
  • Empowered Teams: Limelight provides teams with greater autonomy by giving them access to real-time information and enabling them to manage their workflows independently. The new system of roles and permissions also ensures accountability, reducing the risk of unauthorized changes to sensitive data.

THE PROBLEM

The first step in this project was understanding the challenges that GoLemon’s fulfillment teams faced with the existing process. I conducted interviews with stakeholders from each team involved in the order fulfillment flow, including sourcing, packing, quality assurance (QA), customer support, finance, as well as marketing and engineering teams. The goal was to identify the specific pain points and inefficiencies that Limelight needed to address.

From these conversations, we uncovered the following key challenges:

  • Fragmented Workflow: Teams were using separate tools to manage different parts of the process, leading to misalignment and constant back-and-forth communication. There was no central point for tracking the progress of orders, which caused delays and confusion.
  • Inventory Inaccuracies: With no system in place to sync data across tools, inventory management was manual and prone to errors. When stock levels were inaccurate, it led to issues with sourcing and delays in order fulfillment.
  • Lack of Audit and Accountability: Using Google Sheets for managing sensitive customer and order information led to unauthorized changes and errors. There was no way to restrict access to specific data, and no audit logs to track who made updates or when.
  • Limited Communication Tools: The customer support team relied on Slack to communicate issues related to orders, but Slack lacked the customisation and organisation needed to track specific order details and resolutions.
  • Discount Code Creation: There was no system to effectively manage the creation of discount codes. This created confusion among teams, especially when handling promotions and customer inquiries, leading to inconsistent usage and tracking of discount codes.

OBJECTIVE

To centralise and automate the order fulfillment process, improving operational efficiency, reducing errors, and enhancing communication across teams.

GOALS

  1. Provide real-time visibility of order statuses across all teams.
  2. Integrate with the inventory management system to ensure accurate stock tracking.
  3. Implement roles, permissions, and audit logs to ensure accountability.
  4. Create a tailored communication system to improve internal communication and resolve issues quickly.

SOLUTION

After aligning on these goals, I began the design. I worked closely with the product manager and engineers to ensure the solution addressed the pain points identified during the discovery phase. The solution included the following core features:

Dashboard

The dashboard provides a real-time overview of order statuses, enabling all teams to see what is happening at each stage of the fulfillment process. This eliminates the need for constant updates between teams and ensures everyone has access to the same information.

The dashboard

Order Management System

Limelight’s order management system is divided into clear sections: Sourcing, Processing, Packing & QA, and Order Fulfillment. Each section tracked the status of orders and allowed teams to easily see where bottlenecks were occurring.

The Orders section

The Packing section

Inventory Integration

To solve the issue of inventory inaccuracies, Limelight integrates with the inventory management system. This allows the system to automatically calculate how much stock is needed based on daily orders, reducing errors and ensuring inventory levels are always up to date.

The sourcing section which is synced with inventory

Quantity QA’ed

To further address inventory discrepancies caused by returned items, we introduced a "Quantity QA’ed" column. This ensures that only the items deemed fit for delivery by the QA team are subtracted from inventory, preventing discrepancies and improving inventory reconciliation.

Quantity QA'ed column in the packing section

Manage Payments

The manage payments section automates the process of handling refunds. Previously, customer support manually calculated and issued refunds, but the new system automates these tasks, reducing human error and improving efficiency.

The manage payments section

The invoice section

Roles, Permissions, and Audit Logs

We introduced a robust system of roles and permissions, allowing administrators to restrict access to specific data based on user roles. The audit logs tracks every change made in the system, providing accountability and ensuring that unauthorized changes are easily traced.

Permissions section - Users

Permissions section - Role manager

The Situation Room

The Situation Room replaced Slack as the main communication hub for the customer support team. It allows teams to log and track customer issues in real time, providing greater context and improving collaboration.
